## hist-005 (history)

Has the Reasoning guide always lived at /studio/conversations/reasoning? If not, where was it before and when did it move?

### A0: not run

### A1 (judge: correct)

No, the Reasoning guide has not always lived at `/studio/conversations/reasoning`.

It was previously located at **`/studio-api/conversations/reasoning`** and moved to its current location **between August 1 and August 15, 2026**.

This is confirmed by the documentation history:
- The page at `/studio-api/conversations/reasoning` existed through August 1, 2026, and was removed between August 1-15, 2026.
- The page at `/studio/conversations/reasoning` was added between August 1-15, 2026.
- The deprecated native reasoning page updated its reference from `/studio-api/conversations/reasoning` to `/studio/conversations/reasoning` during the same period.
