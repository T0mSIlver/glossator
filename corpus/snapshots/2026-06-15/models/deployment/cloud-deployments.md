# Cloud

Our models are also available on the third party cloud services, allowing you to deploy them on your preferred cloud provider with flexibility and scalability.

## Cloud Providers {#cloud-providers}

In particular, Mistral's optimized commercial models are available on:
- [Azure AI](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/deployment/cloud-deployments/azure)
- [Amazon Bedrock](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/deployment/cloud-deployments/amazon_bedrock)
- [Google Cloud Vertex AI Model Garden](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/deployment/cloud-deployments/vertex)
- [Snowflake Cortex](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/deployment/cloud-deployments/sfcortex)
- [IBM watsonx](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/deployment/cloud-deployments/ibm-watsonx)
- [Outscale](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/deployment/cloud-deployments/outscale)
